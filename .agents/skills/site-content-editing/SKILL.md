# Site content editing

`./site` is the public front page deployed at https://tau-agent.dev/. Its purpose
is to introduce and advertise Tau: help readers see what the harness makes
possible and why they might want to try it.

- Lead with concrete possibilities and benefits. Write in a friendly, direct,
  informative voice for technically curious readers.
- Avoid legalistic wording, minor operational warnings, and reference-manual
  detail. Link to the relevant docs for prerequisites, edge cases, and full
  setup instructions.
- Configuration sections are feature overviews and glimpses of setup and
  customization, not exhaustive documentation. Keep useful, accurate snippets
  and explain what they enable.
- Stay grounded: no empty hype, invented capabilities, or misleading promises
  about security, defaults, or reliability. Keep qualifications that materially
  affect the reader's understanding, but make them concise.
- Preserve useful links, examples, and page functionality. Distinguish front-page
  promotion from any actual reference documentation before editing.

Use `preview-site` to check desktop and mobile rendering after copy changes.
